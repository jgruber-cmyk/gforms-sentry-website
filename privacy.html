Privacy Policy for gForms-Sentry

1. Camera Access: The app utilizes the camera exclusively for local QR code scanning to verify exam check-in. No image data or video feeds are recorded, saved, or transmitted.

2. Live Guard / Supabase: To synchronize student session status during active exams, the provided name and a generated device identifier are processed temporarily on secure database servers.

3. Data Sharing: No personal data is sold, transferred, or shared with third parties, marketing platforms, or tracking services.
